Tap Pointer is a demo video editor for adding visible tap effects, touch indicators, UI highlights, and voiceover to screen recording videos.

Import a video from Photos or Files, then add tap markers and rectangular highlights exactly where viewers should look. Adjust timing on the timeline, preview the result, and optionally record a voiceover before exporting a high-quality MP4.

Use Tap Pointer for app demo videos, product walkthroughs, bug reports, training clips, tutorials, onboarding videos, and customer support recordings.

Tap Pointer works with videos you choose from your device. It does not draw over other apps, record your screen in the background, or capture touches from other apps. First record your demo video, then import it into Tap Pointer and add the presentation layer afterward.
