Tap Pointer for Mac Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This policy applies to Tap Pointer for Mac, the macOS menu bar app for pointer effects and screen recording. It covers the direct download and the Mac App Store edition.

2. Local recordings and settings

The app processes screen content, microphone narration, optional system audio, pointer movements and click effects on your Mac. Recordings are saved to a folder you choose. Settings and folder-access bookmarks are stored locally. We do not upload these recordings or settings to our servers.

3. Permissions

macOS Screen & System Audio Recording permission is required for screen capture. Microphone permission is required for narration. Optional Accessibility permission enables global stop shortcuts. You control these permissions in System Settings. A security-scoped bookmark retains access to the save folder you select.

4. Purchases and subscription verification

The direct download connects to license.tappointer.com to activate and periodically verify access. It sends the activation code or its activation token. The token and expiry are stored in the Mac Keychain. On the server, activation codes and tokens are stored as hashes. The server stores an encrypted store purchase identifier so it can check whether the subscription is still active. Unused codes expire within 10 minutes. Activation records expire after a year without a successful check.

For the card-free Mac trial, purchase.tappointer.com stores a device key identifier and the trial start and end dates to enforce the seven-day limit. If you choose the Mac subscription, Stripe Managed Payments handles checkout. Our purchase service uses checkout and billing references, encrypted purchase email, payment status, the time and version of the terms you accepted, and activation records to deliver and restore paid access and handle refunds and disputes. Resend uses your purchase email to send a verification code that expires after 10 minutes. The Mac Keychain stores your device key and signed access. The service retains billing and activation records as needed for these purposes.

The Mac App Store edition uses StoreKit to verify and restore the trial and one-time Full Unlock purchase. We do not receive payment card details.

5. Usage information, crash reports and download counts

To understand how many Macs use the app and to fix problems, the Mac app sends a small usage report to license.tappointer.com once a day. It contains a random ID created on your Mac (not linked to your activation, Apple Account or Google Account), the app version, the macOS version, the processor type, the app language and whether it was chosen in Settings, whether this Mac is activated, and whether the app quit unexpectedly last time. We also store the country code (for example, US) that our hosting provider derives from your network address. We do not store the address itself.

If Send crash reports is on in Settings (it is on by default), the report also includes the crash details that macOS provides to the app after a crash, such as the crash type and where in the app it happened. Usage reports never include your name, email address, recordings, screen content, pointer positions or files.

When you download the Mac app from tappointer.com, we count the number of downloads per day and country. We do not store who downloaded it. We keep usage records for about 400 days and crash details for about 180 days. The Mac app includes no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

6. Your control and deletion

Turn off Send crash reports in Settings to stop sending crash details. Choose Disconnect This Mac in Settings to remove its activation record from our service. You can delete recordings in your chosen folder and revoke permissions in System Settings. Removing the app may leave recordings and preferences on your Mac. Apple and Google manage their own purchase records.

7. Contact and changes

If you email support@tappointer.com, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Please avoid including confidential screen content. We may update this policy and will show the effective date above.
