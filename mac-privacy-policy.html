Tap Pointer for Mac Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This policy applies to Tap Pointer for Mac, the macOS menu bar app for pointer effects and screen recording.

2. Local recordings and settings

The app processes screen content, microphone narration, optional system audio, pointer movements and click effects on your Mac. Recordings are saved to a folder you choose. Settings, folder-access bookmarks and license state are stored locally. We do not upload these recordings or settings to our servers.

3. Permissions

macOS Screen & System Audio Recording permission is required for screen capture. Microphone permission is required for narration. Optional Accessibility permission enables global stop shortcuts. You control these permissions in System Settings. A security-scoped bookmark retains access to the save folder you select.

4. Purchases

Apple processes the free 7-day Trial and separate one-time Full Unlock purchase through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to verify and restore transaction entitlements. We do not receive payment card details. The Mac app does not use RevenueCat.

5. No tracking or analytics

The Mac app includes no advertising, behavioral analytics, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not collect personal data from the Mac app.

6. Your control and deletion

You can delete recordings in your chosen folder and revoke permissions in System Settings. Removing the app may leave recordings and macOS app-container preferences on your Mac; you can remove those files yourself. App Store transaction records are managed by Apple.

7. Contact and changes

If you email support@tappointer.com, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Please avoid including confidential screen content. We may update this policy and will show the effective date above.
