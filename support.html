Support

How can we help?

For questions about Tap Pointer, purchases, restore, export, or app demo video editing, contact us at support@tappointer.com.

Import and editing

Tap Pointer works with videos you choose from Photos or Files. Videos can be up to 10 minutes long. If a video is longer, trim it before importing.

Export unlock

Editing and preview are free. High-quality MP4 export is unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. If the unlock is not reflected, use Restore Purchase in the app.

Microphone access

Microphone permission is required only when you record voiceover narration. You can keep the original video audio without recording narration.

Screen recording

Tap Pointer does not record your screen or capture touches from other apps. Record your app demo first, then add tap effects and highlights afterward.
