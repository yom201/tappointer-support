How to record with audio

  1. Open Tap Pointer for Mac and choose a full screen, a single window or a region to record.
  2. Under Sound, select a microphone for your voice. Turn on System audio to include playback from the Mac.
  3. In Effects, set the pointer spotlight, click circles and drag trails you want viewers to see.
  4. Choose a save folder, start recording and click Stop when done. Play the exported MP4 to check the sound and pointer effects.

Recording questions

How do I add my camera to a recording?

Turn on “Include camera” in the recording settings. Select “Adjust Camera…” to choose a camera and set its position and size. Allow camera access in macOS when prompted.

Can I record my voice and the Mac’s audio together?

Yes. The recorder captures the selected microphone. Enable System audio to include sound playing on the Mac. Close unrelated audio sources before recording a demo.

Why is the recording missing audio?

Check the selected microphone and the System audio switch. In macOS Privacy & Security, check Microphone and Screen & System Audio Recording permissions for Tap Pointer. Make and play a short test recording before a longer session.

Can I highlight the cursor without recording?

Yes. Use the live pointer spotlight and click effects while presenting. When you use Tap Pointer’s built-in recorder, the effects appear in the saved MP4 as well.
