Tap Pointer for Mac

Support for your Mac demos

Questions about pointer effects, recording or purchases? Email support@tappointer.com with your macOS version and the steps that caused the issue. Please avoid sending confidential screen content.

Pointer effects

Open Settings from the menu bar. Enable pointer effects and optionally turn on the pointer spotlight. Adjust the size, opacity, left- and right-click effects and drag trail to suit your demo.

Record your screen

Choose a save folder and a display, app or selected region in Settings. Select recording quality and frame rate, then click Start Recording. Allow Screen & System Audio Recording and Microphone access when macOS requests it. Use the visible Stop button to finish. Optional Accessibility permission enables global stop shortcuts.

Free trial and Full Unlock

Activate the free 7-day trial to use all features. After it ends, a separate one-time Full Unlock purchase is required for pointer effects and recording. The current price is shown before purchase. There is no subscription or automatic charge. Your saved recordings remain accessible.

Restore purchases

Use Restore Purchases in Settings while signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Contact support if access is still not restored.

Compatibility and privacy

Requires macOS 15 or later. Recording quality depends on the source display and available performance. Videos remain in the folder you choose. Read the Mac Privacy Policy.
