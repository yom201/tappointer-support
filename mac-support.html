Tap Pointer for Mac

Support for your Mac demos

Questions about pointer effects, recording or purchases? Email support@tappointer.com with your macOS version and the steps that caused the issue. Please avoid sending confidential screen content.

Pointer effects

Open Settings from the menu bar. Enable pointer effects and optionally turn on the pointer spotlight. Adjust the size, opacity, left- and right-click effects and drag trail to suit your demo.

Record your screen

In Settings, choose a save folder and set Capture to Full screen, Single window, or Region. For Single window, click Start Recording and choose the window in the macOS sharing picker. For Region, use Select Region before starting. Allow screen recording when prompted. Allow microphone access if you record narration. Use the visible Stop button to finish. Optional Accessibility permission enables global stop shortcuts.

Activate Mac access

Open the Monthly Plan screen in Tap Pointer on your iPhone or Android phone. If you already subscribe, select Get Mac Activation Code and enter the code in the Mac app. Codes are valid for up to 10 minutes and can be used once.

Check or restore your subscription

Use Restore Purchases in Tap Pointer on the phone signed in to the store account used to subscribe. On Mac, select Check Subscription or enter a new activation code. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the store where you subscribed. Your saved recordings remain accessible after your subscription ends.

Restore a Mac App Store purchase

If your copy shows a free trial or Full Unlock, open Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for that Mac purchase.

Compatibility and privacy

Requires macOS 15 or later. Recording quality depends on the source display and available performance. Videos remain in the folder you choose. Read the Mac Privacy Policy.
